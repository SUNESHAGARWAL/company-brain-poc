# Northwind Logistics - Confidential Information Memorandum (v1)

Northwind Logistics is a regional freight carrier operating 180 tractors across the Midwest.

Financing request: the company is seeking a $12 million senior secured term loan, five-year tenor,
secured by the fleet. Use of proceeds: purchase of 40 new tractors.

Key credit points: long-term customer contracts, EBITDA of $9.1 million, fleet appraisal attached.
